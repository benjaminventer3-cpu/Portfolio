About Me

Background & Skills

From building school labs to studying cybersecurity — here's what I bring to the table.

I'm an IT support professional who doesn't just fix problems — I prevent them. I built and deployed 40+ desktop computers for Barkly East High School, handling the full stack: hardware assembly, OS imaging, software installation, and end-user training for teachers and students.

Right now I'm deep in the ALX Africa Cybersecurity Programme, putting in 6 hours a day, 30 hours a week. I'm learning network security, threat detection, system hardening, and ethical hacking. On the side, I'm self-studying for CompTIA A+ certification to solidify my foundations.

I speak both Afrikaans and English fluently, which means I can support diverse teams and explain complex tech in plain language. I'm a fast troubleshooter, comfortable working autonomously, and I actually enjoy the grind.

Currently based in Barkly East, but willing to relocate immediately anywhere in South Africa. I have a valid driver's licence and I'm ready to move for the right opportunity.
